04 · Measure

Measurements distances and speed on the processed image

Rectify the plane first (lens distortion, perspective). Then set the scale by drawing along something of known length in that plane, and measure other distances in the same plane. For speed, mark a point on the vehicle in one frame, move to a later frame, and mark the same point again.

These checks give indicators to guide an examination, not a verdict. Every technique produces false positives, from fine texture, repeating patterns, heavy recompression and out-of-focus areas, and every technique can miss careful work. Images that have been resized, screenshotted or passed through social media keep far fewer traces. Confirm each finding by examining the image yourself, and record your method.
