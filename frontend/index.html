Forgery Lens gives indicators to guide an examination, not a verdict. Every technique produces false positives, from fine texture, repeating patterns, heavy recompression and out-of-focus areas, and every technique can miss careful work. Images that have been resized, screenshotted or passed through social media keep far fewer traces. Confirm each finding by examining the image yourself, and record your method.
